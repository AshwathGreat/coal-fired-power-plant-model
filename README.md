# Coal-Fired Power Plant — Working Model

A working physical model developed for a School Science Expo to demonstrate the basic working principle of a coal-fired power plant and the conversion of energy into electrical power.

## Working Principle

The project demonstrates:

**Coal → Heat → Steam/Pressure → Turbine → Generator → Electrical Energy → Bulb**

A pressure cooker was used to demonstrate the steam/pressure generation stage. The released pressure was carried through a tube toward a turbine, causing the turbine to rotate.

The rotating turbine was connected to a generator, which converted the mechanical energy into electrical energy and successfully powered a small bulb.

The model also represented the major stages of a conventional coal-fired power plant, including the furnace, turbine, generator, transformer, and power transmission lines.

## Key Features

- Built as a working physical model for a School Science Expo
- Used a pressure-based turbine system
- Demonstrated mechanical-to-electrical energy conversion
- Successfully generated electricity to light a bulb
- Combined a conceptual power-plant model with a functional demonstration
- Demonstrated real-world power generation concepts
